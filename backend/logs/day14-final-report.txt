=========================================
Bug Fix Applied During Validation
=========================================

Issue: "Revenue share by category" was routed to Bar Chart instead of Pie Chart
Root: BAR_KEYWORDS list contained both "category" and "by category", causing
      double-counting. The combined BAR score (1.5 * 2 = 3) beat PIE (2 * 1 = 2)
      whenever a dimension keyword appeared alongside share/distribution words.
Fix:
  1. Split BAR_KEYWORDS → BAR_STRONG_KEYWORDS (top/compare/ranking)
                        + BAR_DIMENSION_KEYWORDS (region/category/product/etc)
  2. New scoring weights:
        TREND  * 2.5
        BAR    = strong*3 + dim*1.2
        PIE    * 4        (higher multiplier for distribution intent)
        KPI    * 2.2
  3. Intent tie-break rule: when PIE keywords + dimension present but NO
     strong comparison keywords (top/compare/rank) → force distribution/pie.
Result: All 7 spec queries now map to their intended chart type.
